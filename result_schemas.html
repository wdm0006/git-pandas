Result schemas¶

Exact column order, index levels and dtype kinds of populated results, pinned by tests/test_schema_contracts.py. Kinds are str, int, float, bool and datetime (UTC); they hold on pandas 2 and 3. Shapes differ between Repository and ProjectDirectory and between populated and empty results; both are listed as they are today.
